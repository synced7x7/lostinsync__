## Badge Rarity & Visual Design System

Implement a consistent visual hierarchy for the five badge rarity categories:

1. **Common** – Simple, clean, minimal design.
2. **Uncommon** – More detailed with enhanced materials and visual accents.
3. **Rare** – Distinctive design with richer details, gradients, and subtle glow.
4. **Legendary** – Highly ornate design with premium effects, intricate details, and stronger illumination.
5. **Mythic** – The highest prestige tier. Exceptional artwork with elaborate detailing, unique visual effects, radiant lighting, and an unmistakably prestigious appearance.

### Design Rules
- Visual quality, complexity, prestige, and exclusivity must increase progressively with each rarity tier.
- A lower-tier badge must NEVER visually outperform a higher-tier badge. Mythic must always be the most impressive, followed by Legendary, Rare, Uncommon, and Common.
- Use distinct visual treatments, materials, colors, lighting, borders, and decorative elements to communicate rarity.
- Maintain the application's dark, premium gaming aesthetic.

### Badge Evolution
For badges with five progression levels, maintain the same core emblem, silhouette, theme, and identity across all five rarities. Evolve the artwork progressively through materials, ornamentation, lighting, textures, and visual effects rather than creating five unrelated designs.

The final result should make every badge immediately recognizable by its category while making Mythic badges feel genuinely exceptional and prestigious.


## Special Category: lostinsync__

Introduce **lostinsync__** as the ultimate achievement category, positioned above Mythic. These badges represent extraordinarily rare accomplishments and the highest level of dedication.

There are only two ways to unlock this category:

1. **Eternal Gamer** – Maintain a gaming journey spanning 50+ years.
2. **Beyond Completion** – Unlock every achievement available in the application.

### Design Direction
These badges must feel beyond ordinary rarity tiers. Use extraordinary, almost celestial artwork, intricate detailing, ethereal lighting, unique visual effects, and exclusive animations.

They must look unmistakably superior to Mythic badges, representing the absolute pinnacle of achievement. Keep their designs exceptionally prestigious and exclusive.
